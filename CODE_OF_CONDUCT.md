# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a professional community space for actuaries and others contributing actuarial tools. We are committed to making participation harassment-free and welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in all discussion, especially code review — critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about actuarial or technical judgment, not personal conflict.
- Be patient with contributors who are new to open source, Git, or GitHub. Most of this community is here first as actuaries, not as software engineers.
- Give credit where due, and avoid presenting others' work as your own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, or associated channels)
- Deliberately submitting incorrect or misleading actuarial logic
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review

## Enforcement

Concerns can be raised privately with any maintainer, or with the ASSA Systems & Technology committee if you'd prefer not to raise it with a maintainer directly. Reports will be handled discreetly. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or (for serious or repeated issues) remove a contributor's access, following the process in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
